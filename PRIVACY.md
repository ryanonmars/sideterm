# SideTerm Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 17, 2026**

SideTerm is a browser side-panel extension that provides a local terminal next to the user's webpages. This Privacy Policy explains what SideTerm does with information when the extension and its optional SideTerm Bridge are used.

## Information SideTerm collects

SideTerm does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information, authentication information, financial information, health information, communications, location information, web history, website content, or user activity to the developer or to third parties.

SideTerm does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or cookies.

## Information stored on your device

SideTerm stores terminal tab names, tab layout, and related workspace preferences in the browser's local extension storage. This information remains on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Terminal input, terminal output, and commands run through SideTerm are passed between the extension and the SideTerm Bridge on your own computer. They are not collected by SideTerm. Programs you run in the terminal retain their own network and privacy behavior.

## Website access

SideTerm does not read webpage content, browsing history, URLs, cookies, or form data. It does not inject scripts into webpages.

## SideTerm Bridge

The optional SideTerm Bridge is a local helper that allows the extension to start and communicate with a shell on your computer. It runs with the permissions of your operating-system user. SideTerm does not receive or store the output of commands outside the local extension workspace.

## Third-party services

SideTerm may link to GitHub to download the SideTerm Bridge release. GitHub's own privacy policy applies when you visit GitHub or download a release. SideTerm does not send your terminal data to GitHub.

## Children's privacy

SideTerm is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when SideTerm changes. The current version will always be available at this URL, with the effective date shown above.
